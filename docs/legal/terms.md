## 6. Collecting and receiving your order

6.1 **Pickup.** For pickup, you and the shop confirm the hand-over by **scanning a QR code**: either the shop shows a code and you scan it with your phone, or you show your code and the shop scans it. Your order page also has a one-time pickup code as a back-up. Keep your codes private; after repeated wrong attempts a code locks.

6.2 **Delivery.** When the rider arrives at the approved delivery point, you and the rider confirm the hand-over by **scanning a QR code**: either the rider shows a code and you scan it, or you show your code and the rider scans it. You must be signed in on your own phone to confirm. Codes expire after a short time and can be used once. The order is completed only when this confirmation is made.

6.3 Delivery times are estimates, not promises. If you cannot confirm the hand-over, contact support. We cannot record an order as delivered when it was not.
