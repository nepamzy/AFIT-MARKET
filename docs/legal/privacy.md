## 2. Cookies, storage and analytics

2.1 **Strictly necessary:** cookies from our sign-in service keep you signed in and protect forms. Your browser also stores settings such as your sound preference and a short note that a banner was dismissed. The installed app may keep pages and files for offline use.

2.2 **Analytics:** we use (a) **Vercel Web Analytics**, which counts visits and pages without cookies and without following you across sites; (b) our own counter, which records a short source tag, device type and install events without cookies and without your name, e-mail or IP address; and (c) **Google Analytics**, which uses cookies and sends usage data to Google to give us reports about visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on. We do not use advertising cookies and do not sell data.

## 3. Who sees your data

- **Vendors** see what they need to prepare your order (the items, the delivery point and your name for the order). They do not get your e-mail unless you give it to them.
- **Riders** see the delivery details for their own jobs only, and the hand-over QR code at the door.
- **Our staff** see only what their role needs. Sensitive access, such as opening an ID photo, is logged.
- **Service providers (processors):** Paystack (payments and bank transfers), Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting and analytics), Brevo (e-mail), Google (analytics), and the push services of your browser. They may only use data to provide their service to us.
- **Authorities:** where the law requires, or to protect people from harm.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some providers store or process data outside Nigeria. We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards and we share only what is needed. Where the NDPA requires safeguards for such transfers we rely on them.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Rider location history is **deleted after 24 hours**.
- Orders, payments, refunds, delivery-fee receipts, ledger and audit records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and disputes, and for any longer period the law requires.
- Support cases are kept while a dispute is possible.
- Your account details are kept while your account is open. After closure we delete or anonymise personal data we no longer need, except records we must keep.
- QR codes expire within minutes and are stored only as hashes.

## 6. Security

We use encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive fields (matric numbers, bank account numbers), role-based access, row-level database security, second-factor protection for staff, append-only audit trails and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure.

## 7. Breaches

If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will take steps to contain it, tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within the time the law requires (currently 72 hours) and, where there is a high risk to you, tell you without undue delay.

## 8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask us to: give you access to your data; correct it; delete it (subject to records we must keep); restrict or object to certain processing; receive your data in a usable format where the law provides; and withdraw consent you have given (this does not affect what we did before). You may also ask not to be subject to a decision made only by automated means. Contact afitmarket01@gmail.com. We reply within the time the law sets. You may complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** if you are not satisfied.

## 9. Closing your account and data requests

Ask for closure or a copy of your data at afitmarket01@gmail.com from the e-mail address on the account (we may ask for proof it is you). We handle requests by hand. Closing an account removes your sign-in, profile details, signature, ID details and alert registrations; records we must keep (orders, payments, refunds, audit) stay in a form that does not need your name beyond what the law requires.

## 10. Location

Location is part of how deliveries work and is used automatically once you have an account, as set out here. A rider's location is collected only while on shift with an active delivery, and is shown only to the customer of that order and, with a stated purpose, to staff (access is logged). The customer's location is shared with the rider only while that rider is on the customer's delivery, and stops when the order is finished. A shop's location is shared with a rider only while that rider is on the way to collect an order from the shop. Phones may limit background tracking, and you can switch location off in your phone's settings, but deliveries may not work without it.

## 11. Children

AFIT Market is for adults (18+). We check the date of birth you give at sign-up and refuse anyone under 18. If we learn an account holder is under 18 we may close the account and delete their data.
