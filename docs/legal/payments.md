# Payments, Delivery Fee and Payouts

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Support: afitmarket01@gmail.com. This notice explains where money goes on AFIT Market.

## 1. What you pay online

At checkout you see the **item total**, the **delivery fee** (if you choose delivery) and your total. You pay the **item total** online through **Paystack**. **Customers pay no platform or service fee.** We never see or store card details. Every payment is confirmed with Paystack before an order goes to the shop.

## 2. The delivery fee

The delivery fee is **not** part of the online payment. You pay it **directly to the rider by bank transfer** after delivery, using the account details on your order page. AFIT Market does not hold the delivery fee and cannot check your bank, so keep your transfer receipt; a receipt is generated on your order page. The fee is not part of the commission base.

## 3. How the shop is paid

3.1 Your online payment is received by us through Paystack and held until the order is completed.

3.2 **24 hours after an order is completed**, we send the shop its payout by bank transfer (Paystack) to the shop's verified bank account. During those 24 hours you can report a problem. **A complaint or refund that is still open holds the payout** until it is settled.

3.3 The payout is the product value of the order **less our commission and less any refund**. If a transfer fails we retry automatically and alert our team.

## 4. Our commission

We charge shops a commission of **4.5% of the product value** of each completed order (after any product discount). Delivery fees are not included. If part of an order is refunded, the commission on that part is returned to the shop. Rounding is to the nearest kobo (half up). The rate at completion is saved with the order. Customers pay no commission.

## 5. Refunds

Refunds go back to the way you paid and are limited to the money received. See the Refund, Return and Dispute Policy. If a shop declines or does not answer within 15 hours, your payment is refunded automatically.

## 6. Receipts and taxes

Your order page shows what you paid and offers a PDF receipt when an order is complete. Vendors are responsible for their own taxes on their sales. We handle our own tax obligations on our commission.
