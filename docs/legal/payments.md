# Payments, Delivery Fee and Payouts

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Support: afitmarket01@gmail.com. This notice explains where money goes on AFIT Market.

## 1. What you pay online

At checkout you see the **item total**, the **delivery fee** (if you choose delivery), the **Paystack charge** and your **checkout total**, before you are sent to Paystack. You pay the total online through **Paystack**. The Paystack charge is the fee Paystack takes for processing the payment; we do not add any platform or service fee of our own. **Several shops, one checkout.** If your cart has items from more than one shop, each shop gets its own order and is paid only for its own items. You may pay for all of them together in one payment, or for each order on its own. We never see or store card details. Every payment is confirmed with Paystack before an order goes to the shop.

**Pay on delivery.** For an order of up to ₦20,000, **other than food**, you may choose at checkout to **pay when you receive it** instead. The shop then prepares your order straight away. When the rider arrives (or you collect at the shop) you check the order and pay through **Paystack** on your phone, and the hand-over is finished only after we have confirmed the payment. Food orders are always paid before the food arrives and cannot be paid on delivery; if a checkout mixes food with other items, the food is paid now and the rest may be paid on delivery. The Paystack charge is shown before you pay. You cannot take the order until it is paid. If you do not pay, nothing is charged and the order goes back to the shop. You may have at most two unpaid pay-on-delivery orders at a time, and we may switch this option off for a customer who repeatedly does not pay. Everything else in this notice applies in the same way: the shop is paid 24 hours after the order is completed.

## 2. The delivery fee

The delivery fee is **part of your one online payment**: it is added at checkout and you pay it together with your items through Paystack. There is nothing more to pay the rider afterwards. The shop pays its own rider. Our commission is taken from the whole payment, delivery fee included.

## 3. How the shop is paid

3.1 Your online payment is received by us through Paystack and held until the order is completed.

3.2 **at midnight (Nigeria time) on the day an order is completed**, we send the shop its payout by bank transfer (Paystack) to the shop's verified bank account. Until then you can report a problem, so please report it the same day you receive your order. **A report that is still open holds the payout** until it is settled.

3.3 The payout is the order total (items and delivery fee) **less our commission**; the shop pays its own rider from it. If a transfer fails we retry automatically and alert our team.

## 4. Our commission

We charge shops a commission of **4.5% of the total** of each completed order (items after any product discount, plus the delivery fee if delivery was chosen). The same rate applies to delivery and pickup. Rounding is to the nearest kobo (half up). The rate at completion is saved with the order. Customers pay no commission.

## 5. Reports

If something is wrong with an order, report it from your order page on the same day you receive it. See the Reports, Returns and Cancelled Orders Policy.

## 6. Receipts and taxes

Your order page shows what you paid and offers a PDF receipt when an order is complete. Vendors are responsible for their own taxes on their sales. We handle our own tax obligations on our commission.
