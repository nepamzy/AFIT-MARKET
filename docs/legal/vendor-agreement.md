# Vendor Agreement

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This agreement is between **NOBS AGENT** (company registration in progress), operator of AFIT Market (the "operator"), and the shop named in your application ("you", "the vendor"). Support: afitmarket01@gmail.com, WhatsApp 0911 5930 445. By applying and ticking acceptance you agree to it.

## 1. Applying and approval

1.1 You apply with your business name, phone number, category, description and pickup location, and accept this agreement. Student vendors give a matric number.

1.2 We may check your details and approve, reject or later suspend a shop, giving reasons. Approval does not guarantee sales. You must be at least 18.

1.3 You are an independent business. Nothing here makes you an employee, partner or agent of the operator.

## 2. What you may sell

You may sell lawful items in the categories we offer (such as food and drinks, clothing, accessories, gadgets and other everyday items). You may not sell illegal, counterfeit, stolen, unsafe or restricted goods, or anything that breaks the Acceptable Use and Safety Rules. Food vendors must meet applicable food-safety and hygiene rules.

## 3. Your listings

You are responsible for true descriptions, photos, prices (in naira), availability and honest preparation times. Ready-made item names and photos we offer as starters are only a convenience; you must make sure they describe what you actually sell, and you set the price.

## 4. Orders: accept or decline

4.1 You see orders that have been paid, and orders the customer has chosen to **pay on delivery** (the order page says so). For each such order you must **accept or decline** it within **15 hours**. If you decline you must give a short reason, which the customer sees. If you do not answer in time, the order is declined.

4.2 You may choose, when you apply, to have orders marked **ready automatically** after the preparation time you set.

4.3 Prepare accepted orders as described, on time, and safely packaged.

## 5. Hand-over

5.1 For **pickup**, you and the customer confirm the hand-over by scanning a QR code: you show yours and the customer scans it, or the customer shows theirs and you scan it. The one-time pickup code is a back-up.

5.2 For **delivery**, you hand the order to the assigned rider. You show a QR code and the rider scans it when collecting.

5.3 You must not hand an order to anyone who cannot confirm it in the app. A hand-over is recorded in the app. For an order paid on delivery you must not hand it over until the app shows **Paid**; the app will not finish the hand-over before that. If the customer does not pay, you keep the goods, nothing is charged to the customer and no payout is made for that order.

## 6. Your own riders

You may add riders for your own shop. Each is given a one-time sign-in code and works only for your shop. You are responsible for the riders you vouch for, for who you give codes to, and for their conduct while delivering your orders. You must not let anyone under 18 deliver.

## 7. Commission

We keep a commission of **4.5% of the total of each completed order** (items after any product discount, plus the delivery fee when delivery is chosen), the same for delivery and pickup. The rate at completion is saved with the order. Customers pay no extra fee.

## 8. Payouts

8.1 We pay you **automatically at midnight (Nigeria time) on the day each order is completed**, by bank transfer (Paystack) to your verified payout account. The amount is the order total (including any delivery fee) less our commission. You pay your own riders from it. An open report holds the payment until it is settled.

8.2 **Payout account.** You choose a bank and enter an account number; we ask the payment provider for the account name and show it for you to confirm. Once verified it cannot be changed unless you contact support, who may unlock it after a check; each unlock is recorded. The number is stored encrypted and staff see only the last four digits.

## 9. Reports and returns

You must cooperate with complaints and returns under the Reports, Returns and Cancelled Orders Policy, reply to statements promptly and honestly, and accept a fair decision.

## 10. Records and taxes

You keep the records the law requires and are responsible for your own taxes and licences.

## 11. Privacy

You may use customer information only to fulfil the order and must not share it, contact customers for marketing, or keep it longer than needed.

## 12. Suspension, termination and appeal

We may suspend or remove a shop for breach, fraud, repeated complaints or safety risks, normally after telling you the reasons and letting you respond. A different person reviews any appeal. We may act at once in urgent cases. You may close your shop at any time after completing open orders.

## 13. Responsibility

You are the seller and are responsible for your goods and their lawful sale. You must indemnify us against claims arising from goods you sell or your breach of this agreement, to the extent the law allows. We are not liable for lost sales caused by outages or events outside our reasonable control.

## 14. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes are first raised with support; if not settled, the courts of Nigeria that have jurisdiction may decide them.
