# Rider Agreement

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This agreement is between **NOBS AGENT** (company registration in progress), operator of AFIT Market, and the rider. Support: afitmarket01@gmail.com, WhatsApp 0911 5930 445. You accept it when you apply and each time we ask you to accept a new version before going active.

## 1. Who may ride

1.1 You must be at least **18**, apply, and be approved. You give your name, phone number, matric number and clear photos of the front and back of your student ID card. We check that the front photo contains the card's QR code; this does not confirm who you are by itself. Approval does not guarantee work.

1.2 A shop may also vouch for its own riders (see the Vendor Agreement). A shop rider works for that shop's orders under this agreement.

1.3 You must give true information. We may suspend you if it is false.

## 2. Your status

You decide when to go active, which delivery offers to accept and how you travel. You are not an employee of the operator and we do not promise a minimum amount of work or income. You are responsible for your own tax and for any licence the law requires for the way you travel.

## 3. How you travel

You may deliver **on foot, by public transport, or by private vehicle**. A car or motorcycle is not required. Licences or documents are needed only for the vehicle you actually use and only where the law requires them. Follow traffic and campus rules.

## 4. Taking deliveries

4.1 You can hold **at most five** accepted active deliveries at once. An order has one active rider at a time.

4.2 Accepting an offer is your choice. Once accepted, complete it or tell support promptly if you cannot. Repeatedly accepting and abandoning jobs may lead to suspension.

4.3 Collect the order from the shop by scanning the shop's QR code (the shop shows it, you scan it). Do not take an order you have not scanned for.

## 5. Location

While you are on shift with an active delivery the app uses your phone's location so the customer can follow the order and so arrival can be detected. It is not collected otherwise. Location history is deleted after 24 hours. A phone that closes or restricts the app may stop sending location.

## 6. Hand-over

6.1 Deliver only to the account holder, at an approved delivery point. If the order is paid on delivery, the app shows that the customer must pay when you arrive: do not hand the order over until the app shows **Paid**; the app will not finish the delivery before that. If the customer does not pay, report it in the app and return the order. When you arrive, confirm the hand-over by **QR code**: you show a code and the customer scans it, or the customer shows a code and you scan it.

6.2 You must not confirm a delivery that did not happen. False confirmations lead to suspension and may be reported to the authorities. If the customer cannot confirm, contact support and do not complete it yourself.

## 7. Delivery fees

The delivery fee shown to the customer is paid **directly to you by bank transfer** from the customer after delivery. You add your bank details in the app; they are stored encrypted and shown to the customer of an order you are delivering. We do not hold or guarantee delivery-fee money. The customer tells the app they have made the transfer and you confirm that you received the amount. You are responsible for checking that your account has been credited before you confirm. A receipt is then generated for the customer.

## 8. Safety and conduct

Follow the Acceptable Use and Safety Rules. Meet customers only at approved delivery points. Do not enter restricted areas without authority. Treat customers, shops and staff with respect. Do not carry out an order you believe is unsafe or unlawful.

## 9. Incidents

Report any incident, accident or problem through the app's issue report. For something serious, also contact afitmarket01@gmail.com or 0911 5930 445. Call the emergency services first when anyone is in danger.

## 10. Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Customer details are for completing the delivery only; do not keep, copy or share them.

## 11. Suspension, termination and appeal

We may suspend or end your access for breach, false claims, safety risks or repeated complaints, normally after telling you why and letting you respond. A different person reviews any appeal. We may act at once in urgent cases. You may stop at any time after finishing your active deliveries.

## 12. Responsibility

You are responsible for what you do while delivering. To the extent the law allows, you must not hold the operator liable for losses caused by your own negligence or breach. We are not responsible for your phone, data costs, clothing or transport.

## 13. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Raise disputes with support first; if not settled, the courts of Nigeria that have jurisdiction may decide them.
