# Acceptable Use and Safety Rules

Last updated: 11 November 2026

These rules apply to everyone using AFIT Market: customers, vendors, riders and staff.

## 1. Be honest

Give true information. Do not pretend to be someone else, use another person's account, or create accounts to avoid a suspension.

## 2. Be respectful and safe

No threats, harassment, discrimination or abuse. Do not share anyone's personal details without their permission. Meet only at approved delivery points and follow the rules of the place you are in. Never put anyone in danger to complete an order; call the emergency services if someone is hurt or at risk.

## 3. Items and listings

Vendors must not list illegal, counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, restricted or misleading items, or anything that cannot lawfully be sold to adults. Food must be prepared safely. Weapons, drugs and adult content are not allowed.

## 4. Payments and fraud

No fake orders, chargeback abuse, false claims of non-delivery or damage, false delivery confirmations, or attempts to pay or be paid outside the platform to avoid fees. The delivery fee is paid to the rider as explained in the Payments notice.

## 5. Protect the platform

Do not try to break, overload or get around the platform's security, scrape its data, copy hand-over QR codes, or use automated tools to place or confirm orders.

## 6. What happens if rules are broken

We may remove content, cancel orders, hold payments while a complaint is open, suspend or close accounts and, where the law requires or to protect people, report to the authorities. We normally tell you why and let you respond first, and a different person reviews any appeal. We may act at once in urgent cases.

## 7. Report a problem

Use the report options in the app, or write to afitmarket01@gmail.com or message 0911 5930 445 on WhatsApp.
