# Refund, Return and Dispute Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Support: afitmarket01@gmail.com, WhatsApp 0911 5930 445. **Nothing here removes a right you have under Nigerian law that cannot lawfully be excluded**, including under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.

## 1. Automatic refund when a shop declines or does not answer

(If you chose to pay on delivery and the shop declines or does not answer, you have paid nothing, so there is nothing to refund.)

After you pay, the shop must accept or decline your order within **15 hours**. If it declines (with a reason you can see on your order page) or does not answer in time, we cancel the order and refund **everything you paid online** automatically to the way you paid. Banks and card networks can take a few working days to show it.

## 2. Problems with an order you received

2.1 Report the problem from your order page. You can do this until the shop has been paid (midnight, Nigeria time, on the day the order was completed, so report it the same day) and, where the law gives you longer, for as long as the law allows.

2.2 Examples: the item was not delivered, was damaged or unsafe, was not what was described, or part of the order is missing.

2.3 We tell the shop and give both sides a chance to explain. Someone not involved in the order reviews the evidence and decides: no action, a partial refund, a full refund, or another fair remedy. You or the shop may appeal; a different reviewer looks at the appeal. Every step is recorded and cannot be edited afterwards.

## 3. What a refund covers

- A refund is limited to money actually received for the order, and refunds together can never exceed it.
- By default a refund covers the **item price** (after any discount) for the affected items. The **delivery fee is paid directly to the rider**, not through us, so we do not refund it from the online payment; if a rider did not deliver, contact support and we will look into it.
- When part of an order is refunded, the commission on that part is returned to the shop.
- Support staff can approve small refunds only within the limit set by our finance team; larger refunds need a finance administrator. The person who asks for a refund cannot approve it.

## 4. Returns

If a return is appropriate, you arrange it with the shop through the order page. There is no automatic restocking or automatic refund on return: the refund is decided under section 2. Goods you simply no longer want, and that match their description, are not covered unless the shop agrees.

## 5. Special cases

- **Paid twice, paid late or the wrong amount:** flagged for review and corrected, never silently kept.
- **Delivery cannot be proved:** we investigate; an order is never recorded as delivered unless it was.
- **Fraud:** knowingly false claims, misdescribed goods and false delivery claims may each lead to action after a fair review.

## 6. After the shop has been paid

The shop is paid at midnight (Nigeria time) on the day the order is completed unless a complaint is open. If we later decide you should be refunded, we refund you and recover the money from the shop under the Vendor Agreement.

## 7. Timing

We aim to decide a complaint within five working days of getting the information we need.
